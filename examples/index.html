Three self-contained interfaces, each built around a different kind of attention. Open one and follow the work.

Intertidal↗

Reading / an independent journal

A contents rail, an original tidal drawing, and an essay about looking a little longer.Try: find “listening”, then follow a passage.

Set↗

Operating / a print-room work desk

A stable worktable and an open job jacket. A small, honest tool for the next handoff.Try: open a job, change its status, apply a note.

Afterimage↗

Exploring / an imagined cultural gathering

An optical poster opens into a running order. Large gestures, explicit destinations.Try: filter the program to sound; open the work.

A note on truth

These are illustrative demonstrations, not live services or case studies. All writing, artwork, jobs, artists and events were authored for the examples. No claims of measured design superiority. No remote assets or fonts. Reading and native navigation work without JavaScript; filters and session-only edits are progressive enhancements.
